## CHARACTERS (By Era)

### EDAT MITJANA (Medieval)
1. Monjo Benedicte: A medieval monk in heavy wool robes standing shivering before the massive frozen gate of The Wall. He holds a flickering candle and a chant book. Snow falls heavily around him. Dark, blue-tinted lighting. Background shows the rough wooden beams of the gate. Serious, fearful expression.
2. Guido d'Arezzo: A scholar monk standing in the snow at the foot of the Ice Wall, showing his hand with musical notes written on the joints (Guidonian hand) to the guard. He wears a heavy fur-lined cowl. The massive ice cliff looms behind him.
3. Trobador Viatger: A ragged bard with a frozen lute strapped to his back, standing at the entrance to Castle Black. He wears worn layers of leather and wool, covered in frost. Hopes to be let in. Mysterious wanderer vibe, cinematic lighting.
4. Sra. Monodia: A solitary woman in plain, dark robes standing against the backdrop of the giant wooden gate. She looks up, singing a single melody. Stark, cold atmosphere. Her breath is visible in the freezing air.
5. Caminant Mozart (Walker): A terrifying White Walker version of Mozart standing just outside the gate. His 18th-century coat is frozen stiff. Glowing blue eyes stare up at the wall. The blizzard swirls violently around him. He holds a conductor's baton like a weapon.
6. Espectre del Rock (Walker): A zombie-like rock star frozen in the snow before the gate. Holding a shattered electric guitar encrusted with ice. His leather jacket is torn. Blue eyes glowing in the dark winter night. Anachronistic horror.
7. Dama de l'Òpera (Walker): A zombie opera singer in a Viking helmet, standing amidst a pile of snow bones at the gate. Her face is pale blue, mouth open in a silent scream. Armor frozen to her skin. Glowing blue eyes.
8. Lord Polifonia (Walker): A ghostly figure with multiple faces shifting in the mist, hovering above the snow near the gate. Represents a chaotic multitude of voices. Ethereal blue glow illuminating the dark gate.
9. Neumes Quadrats: A mystical scribe standing by the portcullis, drawing glowing square musical notes in the air that fade into the snow. He wears heavy travelers' robes. Dark fantasy magic vibe.
10. Gregori el Gran: A commanding Pope-like figure in heavy winter furs over religious vestments, standing confidently before the gate. A white dove perches on his frozen shoulder. The Wall rises infinitely behind him.

### RENAIXEMENT (Renaissance)
11. Palestrina: A dignified composer in velvet robes lined with thick fur, waiting patiently at the gate. He holds a complex sheet music scroll that is fluttering in the wind. The wooden gate is closed tight behind him. Cinematic lighting.
12. Sra. Impremta: A strong woman pushing a cart with a small wooden printing press through the snow towards the gate. She is bundled in heavy wool. The mechanism is frosted over. Steampunk/Renaissance fusion in a winter setting.
13. Madrigalista: A group of five singers standing huddled together for warmth at the foot of the Wall. They wear colorful but muted Renaissance clothes covered in snow. They look up pleadingly at the top of the wall.
14. L'Humanista: A scholar holding a skull and a book, standing alone in the vast snowy wasteland before the gate. He contemplates the skull while waiting to enter. The ice wall reflects the moonlight.
15. Monjo Antic (Walker): A zombie medieval monk clawing at the wooden timbers of the gate. Rotting robes, glowing blue eyes. He holds a broken cross. Scary texture, frostbite details.
16. Caminant Wagner (Walker): A massive, imposing White Walker figure resembling Wagner, wearing a coat of white lion fur. He stands silently staring at the gate, eyes glowing blue. He holds a golden ring. The storm intensifies around him.
17. Lord Sintetitzador (Walker): A horrifying fusion of zombie flesh and frozen cables. He stands at the gate, half-buried in snow. Old wires snake out of the snow like roots. Glowing blue eyes and LEDs.
18. Pianista Virtuós (Walker): A skeletal zombie pianist playing on a piano that is half-buried in a snowdrift outside the gate. His long fingers are ice-blue. Manic expression, glowing eyes.
19. Viola de Mà: A musician playing a 'vihuela' (early guitar), standing shivering by the gate torches. Dressed in elegant court clothes that are ill-suited for the cold. Melancholic atmosphere.
20. Tomás L. de Victoria: A Spanish priest with an intense, spiritual gaze, holding a crucifix. He stands at the Night's Watch gate, illuminated by a single torch. The darkness of the tunnel behind him is ominous.

### BARROC (Baroque)
21. J.S. Bach: J.S. Bach in a heavy coat over his baroque suit, standing sternly at the gate. He holds a quill and scroll. Mathematical geometric shapes made of ice float around him. The Wall looms large.
22. El Clavecí: An ornate harpsichord sitting in the snow before the gate, played by a figure in a heavy wig and furs. The gold leaf of the instrument contrasts with the white snow and dark grey stone.
23. Vivaldi: The "Red Priest" with wild red hair whipping in the wind, playing a violin furiously to keep warm. Standing right in front of the massive wooden doors. Snowstorm ambiance (Winter).
24. Castrati: An androgynous singer in an elaborate, frozen Baroque costume with feathers, standing alone and small against the massive scale of the Ice Wall. Sad, haunting expression.
25. Caminant Jazz (Walker): A zombie jazz musician in a frozen, tattered suit, stumbling towards the gate. He drags a saxophone covered in icicles. Glowing blue eyes. Anachronism in the snow.
26. Sra. Senzillesa (Walker): A zombie peasant woman with a pitchfork, charging towards the camera from the snowy mist. Glowing blue eyes. Simple, torn clothes. The gate is visible in the background.
27. Monjo Gregorià (Walker): A hooded zombie monk, face hidden in shadow except for two glowing blue points. Standing perfectly still in the snow before the gate.
28. Dama del Piano (Walker): A ghostly lady in a torn dress sitting at a piano made of ice. She is right in front of the tunnel entrance. Spooky, translucent blue skin.
29. Baix Continu: A cellist and a keyboardist huddled together under a makeshift shelter near the gate, playing to keep the darkness at bay. Dark baroque painting style, Chiaroscuro lighting from a torch.
30. Rei Sol (Lluís XIV): King Louis XIV in armor and furs, looking like a conquering general arriving at the Wall. He stands tall, demanding entry. Golden details glinting in the cold light.

### CLASSICISME (Classical)
31. Mozart: Young Mozart, shivering but full of energy, writing music on the wooden planks of the gate itself. He wears a red coat covered in snow. Chaotic genius vibe.
32. El Clarinet: A musician holding an early clarinet, standing guard at the gate. He wears a tricorn hat and a heavy military cape. Snow accumulates on his shoulders.
33. Forma Sonata: An allegorical statue-like figure brought to life, standing before the Wall. She holds three scrolls. Draped in classical robes but made of heavy wool for the winter.
34. Haydn: An older, kindly man in a wig and thick cloak, holding a lantern up to inspect the gate. The light reveals the texture of the old wood and ice.
35. Caminant Barroc (Walker): A zombie baroque noble, his powdered wig sliding off his frozen skull. One eye glows blue. He scratches at the ice wall. Rotting finery.
36. Espectre Atonal (Walker): A glitching, abstract ghost entity floating before the gate. It looks like a shard of reality breaking. Glowing blue energy. Dissonant visuals in a snowy landscape.
37. Trobadors (Walker): A horde of zombie bards emerging from the blizzard towards the gate. Broken instruments. Glowing blue eyes. terrifying.
38. Wagner (Walker): Determine zombie Wagner screaming a silent command at the gate. Avalanches crumbling in the background. Blue eyes.
39. Mestre del Pianoforte: An elegant musician playing an early pianoforte situated directly on the snow outside the gate. He wears a heavy greatcoat and top hat. The warm wood of the piano contrasts with the ice.
40. Orquestra Clàssica: A conductor standing on a rock before the gate, commanding an invisible orchestra in the storm. Silhouette against the pale white wall.

### ROMANTICISME (Romantic)
41. Chopin: A pale, sickly Chopin coughing into a handkerchief, standing weakly at the gate. A piano crate is beside him in the snow. Rain and sleet mix. Melancholic grey atmosphere.
42. Wagner (Ally): Wagner in a fur-trimmed beret and heavy cloak, staring up at the top of the Wall with a visionary expression. He looks like he wants to conquer it. Epic cinematic angle.
43. El Virtuós: A demonic violinist (Paganini style) playing on top of one of the gate's fortifications. His hair whips in the wind. Dark, stormy sky.
44. Beethoven: The mature Beethoven, wild grey hair blowing in the blizzard, standing defiantly before the closed gate. He looks like a force of nature. "Der Wanderer" above the sea of fog vibe, but at the Wall.
45. Caminant Racional (Walker): A zombie scientist holding a frozen ruler, measuring the gate blocks. Cold, dead, blue-eyed stare. He ignores the cold completely.
46. Espectre Gregorià (Walker): A swarm of hooded zombie monks scaling the ice wall like spiders. Glowing blue eyes dots in the distance.
47. DJ Modern (Walker): A zombie DJ with frozen turntables set up on a rock in the snow. Tattered modern clothes. Glowing blue eyes. Surreal and scary.
48. Clavecí (Walker): A zombie smashing a harpsichord against the gate doors. Splinters and ice flying. Glowing blue eyes.
49. El Lied: An intimate scene where a singer and pianist perform in the shelter of the gate's tunnel. Candlelight illuminates their faces against the cold blue stone. The singer looks out at the snow.
50. Orquestra Gegant: A wide shot looking down from the top of the Wall. An army of musicians stands in formation in the snow below, waiting to enter. Brass instruments gleam. Epic scale.

### SEGLE XX (20th Century)
51. Stravinsky: Stravinsky in a thick fur coat and round glasses, standing amidst sharp, jagged ice formations near the gate. The ice mimics the angular shapes of his music. Primitive, raw atmosphere.
52. Schoenberg: Schoenberg standing trapped behind a grid of icicles that form a 12-tone matrix. Intense, angst-filled expression. The Wall looms oppressively. Dark blue and orange lighting.
53. John Cage: A man sitting at a snow-covered piano in the middle of the white wasteland, far from the gate. He sits in perfect silence. The wind is the only music. Zen, uncanny.
54. Sintetitzador: A wizard-like musician standing at the gate, connecting patch cables into the ice wall itself, as if hacking the magic. The cables are frozen vines. Glowing amber lights pulse in the ice.
55. Caminant Mozart (Walker): White Walker Mozart, frozen stiff, conducting a blizzard with an icicle. Standing right in front of the camera at the gate. Terrifying blue gaze.
56. Espectre Barroc (Walker): A frozen, floating Baroque zombie drifting through the gate's tunnel. Translucent and icy. Blue eyes.
57. Trobador (Walker): A zombie bard crawling through the deep snow towards the gate, reaching out a frozen hand. Lute crushed. Blue eyes.
58. Verdi (Walker): White Walker General Verdi, on a skeletal horse (or just standing tall), commanding a legion of frozen singers. Top hat and heavy coat. The Wall backdrop is massive.
59. Jazzman: A zombie jazz musician leaning against the cold stone of the gate, playing a frozen saxophone. Noir lighting, long shadows in the snow. Glowing blue eyes.
60. Guitarra Elèctrica: A zombie rocker sliding on his knees in the snow, playing a guitar made of black ice. Lightning strikes the Wall above him. High energy dark fantasy.

# PROMPTS D'ART PER A STRANGER CHRONOS: THE SPLIT

Aquests prompts estan dissenyats per a generar imatges amb IA (Midjourney, DALL-E 3) que encaixin perfectament amb l'estètica "Stranger Things" i la mecànica de pantalla dividida del joc.

---

## 1. FONS DEL MÓN REAL (Real World Background)
**Vibe:** Anys 80, Misteriós, Fred, Tecnològic, Neon Blau.
**Ús:** Part superior de la pantalla.

> **Prompt:**
> `80s synthwave style wide illustration, mysterious suburban street at night, blue and cyan lighting, thick fog, retro bicycle silhouette in the distance, neon blue grid lines overlay on the ground, cinematic lighting, grainy film texture, ominous but calm atmosphere, dark blue sky with stars --ar 16:9 --v 6.0`

---

## 2. FONS DE L'UPSIDE DOWN (Món del Revés)
**Vibe:** Terror, Vermell, Orgànic, Corrupció, Espores.
**Ús:** Part inferior de la pantalla.

> **Prompt:**
> `The Upside Down dimension environment, dark and twisted forest, organic vines covering everything, floating dust particles and spores in the air, intense red lightning storm in the background, dark silhouette of a mind flayer spider monster in the distance, cinematic horror atmosphere, deep red and black color palette, volumetrics, nightmare style --ar 16:9 --v 6.0`

---

## 3. FONS DE LA CARTA (Card Background)
**Vibe:** Artefacte misteriós, VHS gastat, Polaroid fosca.
**Ús:** Fons de les targetes que es mouen. El text anirà a sobre.

> **Prompt:**
> `Texture of an old mysterious 80s VHS cassette tape plastic case, dark worn texture, subtle grunge, with a faint neon red glowing circle in the center, retro sci-fi horror style, analogue noise, vignette edges, dark background for white text legibility --ar 3:4 --v 6.0`

---

## 4. ICONA DEVIDA (Life Icon)
**Vibe:** Cor pixel art o icona de terror retro.
**Ús:** Substituir el cor d'emoji.

> **Prompt:**
> `Pixel art sprite of a red glowing heart, 8-bit arcade style but with a dark horror twist, dripping slightly, neon red glow outline, black background --no text --v 6.0`

---

## 5. ICONES TEMÀTIQUES (Stranger Icons)
**Vibe:** Neon, Objectes Retro, D&D, Minimalista.
**Ús:** Per a representar els conceptes musicals o interfície.

> **Prompt (Pack d'Icones):**
> `Set of neon style icons on a black background, 80s Stranger Things aesthetic. Items include: a red cassette tape, a walkie-talkie, a 20-sided D&D die, a silhouette of a demogorgon, a frozen waffle, a retro bicycle, a polaroid camera. Glowing neon lines, red and blue colors, minimalist vector art style, clean outlines --no text --v 6.0`

---

## 6. MÚSICA I AMBIENT (Audio Prompt)
**Vibe:** Synthwave fosc, Misteri, Tensió, Estil John Carpenter/Stranger Things.
**Ús:** Música de fons per al bucle del joc.

> **AI Audio Prompt:**
> "80s analog synthwave, dark ambient horror, ominous synthesizer arpeggios, pulsing bassline, retro futuristic, John Carpenter keyboard style, Stranger Things OST inspired, mysterious, suspenseful, slow tempo, deep pads, neon noir atmosphere, cinematic, tension building, minimal percussion, heartbeat rhythm, spooky, sci-fi, C Minor key"

## ÀUDIO I MÚSICA

### MÚSICA D'INFILTRACIÓ (TÈNSIO I SILENCI)
*Música ambiental, misteriosa, amb polsos electrònics suaus barrejats amb instruments de l'època.*

1. **Medieval Infiltration:** Ambient Gregorian chants with a low digital hum, dark monastery bells, slow rhythmic wooden percussion.
2. **Renaix Infiltration:** Delicate lute melodies with a glitchy synth pad, echoes of a library, whispers in Latin.
3. **Barroc Infiltration:** Plucked harpsichord with a steady ticking clock sound, high-pitched violin string tension, muffled royal court echoes.
4. **Classic Infiltration:** Minimalist piano notes, symmetrical string patterns, airy palace ambiance with a hint of digital distortion.
5. **Roman Infiltration:** Heavy mechanical gears grinding slow, steam hisses rhythmically, low cello notes mixed with factory noise.
6. **SXX Infiltration:** 1920s low-fi radio static, distorted jazz trumpet echoes from a distance, heavy industrial hum, typewriter rhythm.

### MÚSICA DE BATALHA / BOSS (DUEL I AGRESSIVITAT)
*Música èpica, ràpida, orquestral i moderna alhora.*

1. **Medieval Boss:** High-speed orchestral choir (Dies Irae style), heavy war drums, aggressive organ chords.
2. **Renaix Boss:** Fast-paced harpsichord arpeggios, intense choral polyphony, sudden glitch transitions, sharp strings.
3. **Barroc Boss:** Intense baroque violin solo (Vivaldi style), rapid harpsichord, heavy cinematic bass drops, Vivaldi Winter energy.
4. **Classic Boss:** Dramatic symphony (Beethoven/Mozart Requiem energy), fast woodwinds, loud orchestral stabs, chaotic piano scales.
5. **Roman Boss:** Industrial metal mixed with grand orchestral horns, rhythmic steam-hammer percussion, distorted operatic vocals.
6. **SXX Boss:** Dark techno-orchestral fusion, aggressive atonality, chaotic brass, heavy 1920s jazz subverted by digital chaos.

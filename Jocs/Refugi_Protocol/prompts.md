# Prompts "El Refugi: Fallout Edition"

**Estil General:** Fallout / Wasteland / Retro-Futurism.
**Paleta de Colors:** Monocromàtic (Fòsfor Verd #00ff41 o Àmbar #ffb000) sobre fons negre/fosc. Scanlines CRTs, interfície de terminal antic, brutícia nuclear.

---

## 1. UI & Elements del Joc

### Start Screen (Pantalla d'Inici)
> **Prompt:** A retro-futuristic Fallout-style computer terminal opening screen. Green phosphor text on a curved black CRT monitor. ASCII art skull or radiation symbol in the center. Text: "REFUGI PROTOCOL". "ENTER PASSWORD" blinking cursor. Glitchy, old tech vibe. High contrast green and black.

### Game Layout (Booth Window)
> **Prompt:** First-person view from inside a concrete bunker booth looking out through a thick, scratched, dirty bulletproof glass window. The interior frame is rusted metal with green illuminated buttons and a microphone. Keep the center clear for the character. Outside is dark and nebulous.

### Era Selector Icons (Pip-Boy Style)
> **Prompt:** A set of simple pixel-art or vector-line green icons on black background, Pip-Boy style:
> 1.  **Medieval:** A stylized knight helmet or castle tower.
> 2.  **Renaissance:** Vitruvian Man minimalist sketch.
> 3.  **Baroque:** A fancy wig or a harpsichord silhouette.
> 4.  **Classicism:** A greek temple column.
> 5.  **Romanticism:** A lightning bolt striking a tree or a dramatic ghost.
> 6.  **20th Century:** An atom symbol or a radio tower.

## 2. Backgrounds (Escenaris per Època)
*Nota: Els fons es veuran a través de la finestra del búnquer. Han de semblar "El Món Exterior" corresponent a cada època però en versió "Wasteland" / Apocalíptica.*

**Estil:** "Post-Apocalyptic Historical". Realistic but grim, dark atmosphere, desolate.

1.  **Edat Mitjana (The Ruined Castle):**
    > **Prompt:** A dark, foggy medieval wasteland. In the distance, the burning ruins of a stone castle. Crows flying in the grey sky. Muddy ground, dead trees. Realistic, cinematic, gloomy.
2.  **Renaixement (The Abandoned Plaza):**
    > **Prompt:** An abandoned Italian Renaissance plaza with cracked marble pavement. A fallen statue of David. Overgrown with toxic glowing moss. Strange Renaissance machinery rusted in the corner. Sunset lighting.
3.  **Barroc (The Gilded Ruins):**
    > **Prompt:** The ruins of a Versailles-style garden. Golden gates twisted and broken. Fountains filled with green sludge. Overgrown hedges forming a dark maze in the background. Foggy and mysterious.
4.  **Classicisme (The Fallen Temple):**
    > **Prompt:** A neoclassical landscape with shattered white marble columns. A Greek temple structure collapsing. The sky is stormy and purple. Debris of statues on the ground.
5.  **Romanticism (The Stormy Cemetery):**
    > **Prompt:** A gothic cemetery in a heavy storm. Crooked tombstones, dead weeping willow trees, old iron fences. A distant jagged mountain peak. Very dark, dramatic, shadowy.
6.  **Segle XX (The Nuclear City):**
    > **Prompt:** A destroyed 1940s city skyline. Art Deco skyscrapers with broken windows. Smoke rising. Rubble in the streets. Noir atmosphere, grey and industrial.

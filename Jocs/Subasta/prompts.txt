# PROMPTS PER A LES IL·LUSTRACIONS DE LA SUBASTA (SUBASTA/IMG)

Copia i enganxa aquests prompts per generar els "Quadres" de la subhasta.
**Estil OBLIGATORI:** "Oil painting on canvas, ornate gold frame, frontal view, centered, isolated on white background. No 3D objects outside the frame. The painting features a visible elegant inscription."

---

2. **Llaüt:** Oil painting of a medieval lute. Ornate gold frame. Frontal view. Inscription reading "MUSICA DIVINA" in gothic script.
3. **Corona Reial:** Oil painting of a golden crown. Ornate gold frame. Frontal view. Inscription reading "REX GLORIAE" in gothic script.

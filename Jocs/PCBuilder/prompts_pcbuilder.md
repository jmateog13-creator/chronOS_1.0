# Prompts for PC Builder: Setup Històric

**Overall Style:**
"Historical Tech Fusion". High-quality, realistic, or 3D render style of computer components (Motherboards, CPUs, GPUs) but heavily customized with the aesthetic materials and art styles of their respective historical eras.
*   **Background:** Clean, neutral tech product studio lighting (dark grey or white).
*   **Perspective:** Isometric or Top-down product shot.

---

## 1. Motherboards (MOBO)
*   **Medieval:** "Theocentric Socket" (`mobo_medieval.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A rustic computer motherboard made of dark oak wood and iron bands. The circuits look like illuminated manuscript lines in gold leaf. The CPU socket is shaped like a gothic cathedral rose window.
*   **Renaissance:** "Humanism Pro" (`mobo_renaissance.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A beautiful marble and gold motherboard. The capacitors are shaped like small greek columns. The design follows perfectly symmetrical proportions (Golden Ratio).
*   **Baroque:** "Basso Continuo Ultra" (`mobo_baroque.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A heavy, luxurious motherboard covered in gold leaf and complex ornaments. Extremely detailed, almost chaotic wiring but with a strong, thick golden foundation line at the bottom.
*   **Classicism:** "Sonata Form Arch" (`mobo_classicism.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A very clean, balanced motherboard. Deep blue and silver. Strictly symmetrical layout. No clutter, everything is in perfect order.
*   **Romanticism:** "Individualism Extreme" (`mobo_romanticism.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A jagged, dramatic motherboard. Dark PCB with storms painted on it. Wiring looks wild and organic, breaking out of standard pathways.
*   **Segle XX:** "Atonal Board" (`mobo_sxx.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A chaotic, glitch-art motherboard. Components are placed randomly without an apparent center. Neon colors, industrial metal.

## 2. CPUs (Processors)
*   **Medieval:** "Gregorian Single-Core" (`cpu_medieval.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A square CPU processor made of parchment and wax seal. It has a single large monk symbol or a neume note in the center. Very simple, ancient tech.
*   **Renaissance:** "Imitative Pulse" (`cpu_renaissance.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A CPU engraved with the Vitruvian Man. It looks clean, copper and silver, representing human perfection.
*   **Baroque:** "Motoric Rhythm 4GHz" (`cpu_baroque.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A CPU that looks like a complex clockwork mechanism with golden gears. It signifies constant, unstopping motion.
*   **Classicism:** "Symmetric 4+4 Core" (`cpu_classicism.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A CPU with a perfectly square, mirrored design. 8 distinct cores visible in a neat grid. Simple, elegant branding "Equilibrium".
*   **Romanticism:** "Rubato Unlocked" (`cpu_romanticism.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A CPU that appears to be pulsating or unstable. Vibrant red and black colors. Symbolizes breaking tempo and rules.
*   **Segle XX:** "Polyrhythm Glitch" (`cpu_sxx.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A CPU that looks digital, fragmented, or pixelated. Modern silicon aesthetics but broken or rearranged. Cybernetic look.

## 3. GPUs (Graphics Cards)
*   **Medieval:** "Monodia Integrated" (`gpu_medieval.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A very basic, small graphics card made of stone and wood. It has a single fan that looks like a wooden mill wheel. No extra power cables.
*   **Renaissance:** "Polyphony x4" (`gpu_renaissance.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A graphics card with 4 sleek fans, interlaced like complex vocal lines. Elegant floral engravings on the backplate. White and Gold color scheme.
*   **Baroque:** "Harpsichord Gold" (`gpu_baroque.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A massive graphics card that resembles the inside of a Harpsichord. Gold strings, painted landscapes on the casing, pearl keys on the side.
*   **Classicism:** "Melody+ Ti" (`gpu_classicism.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A high-end graphics card with a very clear, dominant single fan design (Melody) supported by a subtle heatsink (Accompaniment). Polished aluminum finish.
*   **Romanticism:** "Orchestra RTX 4090" (`gpu_romanticism.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A humongous, thick graphics card. It looks powerful and intimidating. Triple fans that look like timpani drums. Dark, grandiose aesthetic.
*   **Segle XX:** "Noise Synth" (`gpu_sxx.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A graphics card that looks like a synthesizer or an industrial machine. Exposed wires, vacuum tubes mixed with modern fans. Steampunk meet Cyberpunk.

## 4. Style (Cooling & RGB)
*   **Medieval:** "No-RGB Austere" (`style_medieval.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A PC case cooling system that is just a simple, unadorned iron grate. Zero lights, very dark and serious. A single candle might be sitting on top.
*   **Renaissance:** "Harmony White Flow" (`style_renaissance.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A PC cooling system with white liquid coolant tubes that look like marble veins. Soft, warm divine light (not harsh RGB).
*   **Baroque:** "Custom Loop Gold RGB" (`style_baroque.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A water cooling loop where the liquid is literal liquid gold. The pipes are twisted and dramatic. Bright, contrasty lighting (Chiaroscuro effect inside the case).
*   **Classicism:** "Clean Minimalist" (`style_classicism.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A PC build with neat cable management, soft white or light blue lighting. Very open airy space, Greek temple aesthetics in a modern case.
*   **Romanticism:** "Passion Red Liquid" (`style_romanticism.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A liquid cooling system with blood-red fluid. Fog or steam effects inside the case. Intense, emotional lighting.
*   **Segle XX:** "Cyberpunk Neon" (`style_sxx.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A futuristic PC case interior. Laser lights, neon pink and green cables. Dystopian high-tech feel.

## 5. Full Setups (Victory Screens)
*   **Medieval:** "The Scriptorium Battlestation" (`pc_full_medieval.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A full PC gaming setup inside a stone monastery cell. The monitor is framed in heavy oak wood with iron bolts. The keyboard replicates a typewriter or organ keys. A candle is providing dim lighting. The PC case looks like a reliquary box. Dark, moody, brown and gold tones.
*   **Renaissance:** "The Da Vinci Workstation" (`pc_full_renaissance.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A full PC setup on a messy inventor's desk. The monitor has a golden frame with perfect proportions. Scrolls and compasses are scattered around. The PC case is a masterpiece of marble and copper gears. Ideally lit by natural sunlight from a window. Clean, warm, intellectual atmosphere.
*   **Baroque:** "The Royal Gold Rig" (`pc_full_baroque.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** An insanely opulent PC setup. Everything is gold. The monitor has heavy curtains around it like a theater stage. The PC case is overflowing with ornaments and golden light. It looks like it belongs in the Palace of Versailles. Dramatic lighting, excessive luxury.
*   **Classicism:** "The Enlightened Desk" (`pc_full_classicism.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A perfectly symmetrical, clean PC setup. White and pastel blue colors. The desk is neat, with a bust of Mozart or a Greek column nearby. The PC case is a simple, elegant rectangular prism with soft lighting. Order, balance, and logic.
*   **Romanticism:** "The Stormy Gamer" (`pc_full_romanticism.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A dramatic PC setup on a cliff edge or in a dark, stormy room. The PC case is glowing red and black, looking powerful and emotional. Mist or fog surrounds the desk. It feels intense, individualistic, and epic.
*   **Segle XX:** "The Future Lab" (`pc_full_sxx.png`)
    *   **Prompt:** A chaotic, high-tech lab setup. Multiple monitors, wires hanging everywhere, neon lights. The PC case looks like a prototype synthesizer or a mainframe. Glitch art aesthetics on the screens. Industrial, noisy, and colorful.

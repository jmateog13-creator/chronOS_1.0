## 4. MÚSICA I SO (AUDIO)

### BANDA SONORA (Ambient Nòrdic)
**Prompt:** Nordic Viking ambient music, slow and mystical. Instruments: Tagelharpa (bowed lyre), deep frame drums (soft rhythmic heartbeat), distant throat singing drone. Ethereal choir pads. Atmospheric, cold but magical. No intense battle drums, just puzzle/contemplation mood.
**Referència:** Wardruna, Heilung (parts tranquil·les), God of War (parts d'exploració).

### EFECTE DE SO: PEDRA (Stone Click)
**Prompt:** Heavy stone sliding on rock, followed by a solid deep "thud" or "clack". Realistic granite sound.

### EFECTE DE SO: RITUAL (Màgia)
**Prompt:** Magical shimmering sound, crystalline chimes, ethereal whoosh, deep bass rumble rising. Success sound.

--- MUSICAL CONCEPTS (ABSTRACT) ---

[CONCEPT_MONODIA] (Medieval)
PROMPT:
A minimalist technical blueprint drawing representing "Monodia" (Single Melody). A single, clean, flowing waveform line centered on a blueprint grid background. White chalk style. Technical schematic feel. No extra noise. --ar 1:1

[CONCEPT_POLYPHONY] (Renaissance)
PROMPT:
A minimalist technical blueprint drawing representing "Polyphony" (Multiple Melodies). Three distinct flowing lines interweaving harmoniously like a braid or DNA helix, on a blueprint grid background. White chalk style. Technical schematic. --ar 1:1

[CONCEPT_BASSO_CONTINUO] (Baroque)
PROMPT:
A minimalist technical blueprint drawing representing "Basso Continuo". A thick, solid, straight horizontal line at the bottom, with pillars or smaller lines growing upwards from it. Representing foundation and support. Blueprint grid background. White lines. --ar 1:1

[CONCEPT_CONTRAST] (Baroque)
PROMPT:
A minimalist technical blueprint drawing representing "Contrast". A design split in half: one side is dense with many lines, the other is empty or has a single thin line. Representing loud/soft or light/dark. Blueprint grid background. White lines. --ar 1:1

[CONCEPT_FORM_ABA] (Classicism)
PROMPT:
A minimalist technical blueprint drawing representing "Sonata Form" (ABA). Three geometrical shapes in a row: A Circle, a Triangle, and a Circle (A-B-A structure). Symmetrical and balanced. Blueprint grid background. White lines. --ar 1:1

[CONCEPT_ROMANTICISM] (Expression)
PROMPT:
A minimalist technical blueprint drawing representing "Romantic Expression". A chaotic, explosive splash or jagged line breaking out of a strict square box. Representing breaking rules and emotion. Blueprint grid background. White lines. --ar 1:1

[CONCEPT_ATONAL] (20th Century)
PROMPT:
A minimalist technical blueprint drawing representing "Atonality". Scattered geometric points and disconnected short lines floating without a center or gravity. Abstract and random. Blueprint grid background. White lines. --ar 1:1

[CONCEPT_ELECTRONIC] (20th Century)
PROMPT:
A minimalist technical blueprint drawing representing "Electronic Music". A square wave and a sawtooth wave (synthesizer shapes) or a circuit board node pattern. Technical and digital. Blueprint grid background. White lines. --ar 1:1

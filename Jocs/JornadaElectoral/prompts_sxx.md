# Prompts for Jornada Electoral - Segle XX (20th Century)

**Overall Style Reference:**
Match the style of the existing "Jornada Electoral" assets (e.g., `ren_polifonia.png`, `med_cantelli.png`).
*   **Style:** "Hand-drawn Political Caricature" or "Satirical Election Illustration".
*   **Medium:** Ink and watercolor / Digital Illustration that looks like a printed ballot portrait.
*   **Framing:** Waist-up or Bust portrait, facing forward or slightly side-profile (Candidate pose).
*   **Tone:** Humorous but recognizable historical archetypes.
*   **Background:** Simple or transparent (white/paper texture).

## 1. Professor Robert Atonal
*   **Filename:** `sxx_atonal.png`
*   **Key Concept:** "The Breaker of Rules".
*   **Visual Description:** A stern, intense academic (archetype: Arnold Schoenberg or early modernist).
    *   **Attire:** Rigid suit, round glasses, perhaps a bow tie.
    *   **Action:** Holding a baton like a weapon, or breaking a "Treble Clef" in half with his hands.
    *   **Expression:** Serious, revolutionary, uncompromising.
*   **Slogan Context:** "A baix la tirania de la tònica!" (Down with the tyranny of the tonic!).

## 2. Sra. Úrsula Dodecafonia
*   **Filename:** `sxx_dodecafonia.png`
*   **Key Concept:** "Mathematical Equality".
*   **Visual Description:** A sophisticated, avant-garde woman of the 1920s/30s.
    *   **Attire:** Geometric patterns allowed, bobbed hair, pearls (but ordered mathematically).
    *   **Action:** Holding a voting ballot or a grid that has exactly **12** checked boxes, showing them off proudly.
    *   **Expression:** Calculated, precise, slightly haughty.
*   **Slogan Context:** "12 notes, 12 vots, igualtat total!" (12 notes, 12 votes, total equality!).

## 3. Senador Salvador Sorolls
*   **Filename:** `sxx_sorolls.png`
*   **Key Concept:** "The Noise Maker" (Futurism).
*   **Visual Description:** An eccentric, energetic Futurist (archetype: Luigi Russolo).
    *   **Attire:** A mix of a suit and industrial gear/lab coat.
    *   **Action:** Shouting into a megaphone that is attached to a strange wooden box machine (Intonarumori).
    *   **Expression:** Wild-eyed, shouting, chaotic energy.
*   **Slogan Context:** "El soroll també és música!" (Noise is also music!).

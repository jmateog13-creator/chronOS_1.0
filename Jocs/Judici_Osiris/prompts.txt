# PROMPTS D'IMATGES - EL JUDICI D'OSIRIS
# Estil: Epic Fantasy Art, Digital Painting, semi-realista però estilitzat, "Dark Egyptian Fantasy".
# Públic: Adolescents (3r ESO). Èpic i impressionant, lleugerament fosc/misteriós però no "gore" ni terrorífic.
# Format: 16:9 per fons, 1:1 o Vertical per personatges.

## 1. FONS (BACKGROUNDS)
**Nom del fitxer**: `bg_courtroom.png`
**Descripció**:
> A wide, majestic view of the "Hall of Judgment" (Duat) in Ancient Egypt.
> Dark, atmospheric lighting with golden accents.
> In the center, a large empty space for the gods to appear.
> On the sides, massive pillars with hieroglyphs glowing faintly in turquoise.
> In the background, a starry cosmic void visible through arches.
> The floor is polished obsidian reflecting the scene.
> Cinematic lighting, mystic fog.

## 5. MÚSICA DE FONS (AUDIO)
**Nom del fitxer**: `bg_judgment.mp3`
**Descripció**:
> A continuous, epic and atmospheric track for the "Hall of Judgment".
> **Style**: Ancient Egyptian Dark Fantasy / Cinematic Ambient.
> **Instruments**: Deep drones (pads), distant frame drums (rhythmic but slow), Ney flute or Duduk (mournful/mysterious melody), occasional chimes.
> **Mood**: Solemn, epic, mysterious, slightly tense. It should feel like a serious spiritual trial in a massive temple.
> **Reference**: "The Mummy" soundtrack, "Assassin's Creed Origins" ambient, "Raiders of the Lost Ark" map room.

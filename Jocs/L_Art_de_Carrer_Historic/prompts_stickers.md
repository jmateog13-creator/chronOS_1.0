# Prompts per a Generar els Stickers (Estil Street Art / Cartoon)

Utilitza aquests prompts en una eina com Midjourney, DALL-E 3 o Leonardo.ai.
**Estil General:** "Die-cut sticker, white border, vibrant vector art, street art style, graffiti influence, cartoonish, colorful, high contrast, isolated on white background."

---

## 1. Edat Mitjana (Medieval)
1.  **Monodia (Concepte):** "Die-cut sticker representing 'Monody' (single melody), a single bold musical line flowing alone, simple vector art."
2.  **Cant Gregorià (Concepte):** "Die-cut sticker of a monk singing mouth with a speech bubble containing square notes (neumes), cool cartoon style."
3.  **Monestir:** "Die-cut sticker of a romanesque monastery with a cloister, stone texture, cartoon style."
4.  **Monjo Guido:** "Die-cut sticker of a medieval monk (Guido d'Arezzo) pointing at a musical hand, funny cartoon style."
5.  **Viola de Roda:** "Die-cut sticker of a hurdy-gurdy (viola de roda) instrument, wooden texture, vibrant cartoon style."

## 2. Renaixement (Renaissance)
1.  **Polifonia (Concepte):** "Die-cut sticker representing 'Polyphony', multiple colored musical lines intertwining harmoniously, abstract vector style."
2.  **Humanisme (Concepte):** "Die-cut sticker representing 'Humanism', a stylized human head profile with a lightbulb or galaxy inside, intellectual street art style."
3.  **Impremta:** "Die-cut sticker of a Gutenberg printing press, wooden and metal parts, ink splatters, cartoon street art style."
4.  **Home de Vitruvi:** "Die-cut sticker of the Vitruvian Man (Da Vinci) but stylized as a cool vector character, circle and square frame."
5.  **Madrigal (Coral):** "Die-cut sticker of a group of 4 singing mouths or faces, representing a choir, colorful."

## 3. Barroc (Baroque)
1.  **Baix Continu (Concepte):** "Die-cut sticker representing 'Basso Continuo', a heavy bold Bass Clef with numbers underneath, graffiti style."
2.  **Contrast (Concepte):** "Die-cut sticker representing 'Contrast', split design half black/white or half loud/soft (forte/piano), dramatic style."
3.  **Violí:** "Die-cut sticker of a classic violin with a bow, elegant wood texture but with a graffiti edge, cartoon style."
4.  **Perruca Blanca:** "Die-cut sticker of a large white powdered wig (Mozart/Bach style), ornate and fluffy, funny cartoon style."
5.  **Orgue de Tubs:** "Die-cut sticker of a grand pipe organ with metallic pipes, dramatic angle, cartoon style."

## 4. Classicisme (Classical)
1.  **Melodia Acompanyada (Concepte):** "Die-cut sticker representing 'Melody with Accompaniment', one distinct golden wavy line floating above simple block chords, vector style."
2.  **Equilibri/Ordre (Concepte):** "Die-cut sticker of a perfectly balanced scale or symmetrical geometric musical pattern, clean lines, classical vibe."
3.  **Piano:** "Die-cut sticker of a fortepiano (early piano), wooden, elegant, cartoon vector style."
4.  **Quartet de Corda:** "Die-cut sticker representing 4 string instruments (2 violins, viola, cello) together, cartoon group style."
5.  **Mozart:** "Die-cut sticker of Mozart conducting, red coat, white wig, dynamic pose, funny cartoon character."

## 5. Romanticisme (Romanticism)
1.  **Rubato (Concepte):** "Die-cut sticker representing 'Rubato', a metronome melting like Salvador Dali or a wavy clock, flexibility of time, street art."
2.  **Virtuosisme (Concepte):** "Die-cut sticker representing 'Virtuosity', hands playing piano extremely fast with fire/sparks, dynamic motion blur."
3.  **Gran Piano:** "Die-cut sticker of a grand piano, black and shiny, open lid, dramatic, cartoon style."
4.  **Beethoven:** "Die-cut sticker of Beethoven looking intense and wild hair, holding a quill, graffiti character style."
5.  **Tempesta:** "Die-cut sticker of a storm cloud with lightning bolts and musical notes, dramatic energy, street art style."

## 6. Segle XX (20th Century)
1.  **Atonalisme (Concepte):** "Die-cut sticker representing 'Atonality', musical notes floating chaotically without a staff, erratic and abstract, graffiti style."
2.  **Dodecafonisme (Concepte):** "Die-cut sticker with the number 12 and a matrix of notes, mathematical music style, cool vector."
3.  **Guitarra Elèctrica:** "Die-cut sticker of a red electric guitar (Stratocaster style), rock n roll vibe, flames, cartoon style."
4.  **Ràdio Antiga:** "Die-cut sticker of a vintage wooden radio set, retro style, cartoon vector."
5.  **Saxòfon:** "Die-cut sticker of a golden saxophone, shiny brass, jazz vibes, street art style."

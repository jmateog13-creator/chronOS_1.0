# Prompts Artístics: L'Arquitecte Sonor
**Estil General:** "Technical Architectural Blueprint".
Totes les imatges han de seguir aquesta estètica:
- **Fons:** Blau marí fosc (Royal Blue / Blueprint Blue #0044cc).
- **Línies:** Blanques, fines, precises (tècnica de delineant).
- **Detalls:** Cotas, notes manuscrites, graella de fons subtil.
- **Vibe:** Premium, històric, tècnic però artístic.

---

## 1. UI & Elements Comuns
Aquests elements serveixen per a la interfície general.

**Fons General (Seamless Loop):**
> A dark blue architectural blueprint paper texture with a precise white grid graph pattern. High resolution, minimalist, clean technical drawing background. Seamless texture.

**La Grua (Sprite):**
> A white outline architectural drawing of a construction crane hook and cable, hanging dynamically. Blueprint style, technical sketches, white lines on dark blue background. Minimalist.

**L'Arquitecte (Avatar):**
> A friendly cartoon construction worker wearing a yellow hard hat and holding rolled-up blueprints under his arm. Simple, clean lines, flat style character. He is giving a "thumbs up". The style is "Blueprint Cartoon", white lines on blue background but with a fun, approachable personality.

## 2. Les Torres (Per Èpoques)
Per a cada època, necessitem dos tipus d'imatges:
1.  **Parts Modulars:** 4 blocs separats (Ciment, Estructura, Façana, Decoració).
2.  **Torre Completa:** La torre finalitzada i majestuosa (per al modal de victòria).

### Època 1: Edat Mitjana (La Fortalesa)
**Concepte:** Castell de pedra robust, romànic.
> **Full Tower:** "Architectural blueprint drawing of a medieval stone fortress tower. Romanesque style, thick stone walls, small windows, sturdy base. White technical lines on blue background. Detailed, majestic."
> **Parts:**
> 1.  (Base) "Blueprint drawing of a heavy medieval stone foundation, thick rocks."
> 2.  (Body) "Blueprint drawing of a medieval wall section with small archer windows (lower section)."
> 3.  (Facade) "Blueprint drawing of a medieval wall section with arched windows and torches (upper section)."
> 4.  (Decoration) "Blueprint drawing of castle battlements with flags and a stone roof tower."

### Època 2: Renaixement (El Duomo)
**Concepte:** Catedral de Florència, cúpula, simetria.
> **Full Tower:** "Architectural blueprint drawing of a Renaissance cathedral tower with a large Brunelleschi dome. Perfect symmetry, arches, columns. White lines on blue background."
> **Parts:**
> 1.  (Base) "Blueprint drawing of an elegant Renaissance marble step foundation."
> 2.  (Body) "Blueprint drawing of a building floor with rusticated stone work (lower section)."
> 3.  (Facade) "Blueprint drawing of a building floor with symmetrical arched windows and pilasters (upper section)."
> 4.  (Decoration) "Blueprint drawing of a magnificent ribbed dome with a lantern on top."

### Època 3: Barroc (El Palau)
**Concepte:** Versalles, ornaments, luxe desmesurat.
> **Full Tower:** "Architectural blueprint drawing of a Baroque palace facade. Extremely ornate, complex decorations, statues, balustrades, grandeur. White lines on blue background."
> **Parts:**
> 1.  (Base) "Blueprint drawing of a grand palace entrance with ornate stairs."
> 2.  (Body) "Blueprint drawing of a floor with decorated columns and elaborate window frames (lower level)."
> 3.  (Facade) "Blueprint drawing of a floor with balconies and statues (upper level)."
> 4.  (Decoration) "Blueprint drawing of a mansard roof with gold leaf details drawn in lines and statues."

### Època 4: Classicisme (El Temple)
**Concepte:** Partenó, columnes gregues, ordre pur.
> **Full Tower:** "Architectural blueprint drawing of a Neoclassical temple building. Clean doric columns, triangular pediment, rationality, white marble implied. White lines on blue background."
> **Parts:**
> 1.  (Base) "Blueprint drawing of a stylobate (stepped platform) foundation."
> 2.  (Body) "Blueprint drawing of the lower part of fluted classical columns."
> 3.  (Facade) "Blueprint drawing of the upper part of fluted columns with capitals."
> 4.  (Decoration) "Blueprint drawing of a triangular pediment and entablature with friezes."

### Època 5: Romanticisme (La Torre Gòtica)
**Concepte:** Neogòtic, misteri, nexe amb la natura, punxegut.
> **Full Tower:** "Architectural blueprint drawing of a dramatic Gothic Revival tower. Tall spires, pointed arches, gargoyles, slightly crumbling or overgrown ruin aesthetic. White lines on blue background."
> **Parts:**
> 1.  (Base) "Blueprint drawing of ONLY the bottom foundation of a gothic tower. Heavy stone blocks covered in creeping ivy and vines. No windows, just solid base and roots. Isolated element."
> 2.  (Body) "Blueprint drawing of a single floor section of a gothic tower. Features tall, narrow lancet arches (pointed windows) and vertical buttresses. Middle section only. Isolated element."
> 3.  (Facade) "Blueprint drawing of an upper floor section of a gothic tower. Features a large, detailed circular Rose Window in the center. Intricate tracery. Upper section only. Isolated element."
> 4.  (Decoration) "Blueprint drawing of ONLY the sharp roof spire of a gothic tower. A tall pointed narrow roof with a cross or finial on top and small gargoyles on the corners. Roof only. Isolated element."

### Època 6: Segle XX (El Gratacels)
**Concepte:** Bauhaus/Modernisme, acer i vidre, gratacels.
> **Full Tower:** "Architectural blueprint drawing of a modern 20th-century skyscraper. Steel beams, glass curtain wall, abstract geometry, Bauhaus style. White lines on blue background."
> **Parts:**
> 1.  (Base) "Blueprint drawing of a concrete and steel modern foundation."
> 2.  (Body) "Blueprint drawing of a grid of steel frames and glass windows (lower)."
> 3.  (Facade) "Blueprint drawing of a grid of steel frames and glass windows (upper)."
> 4.  (Decoration) "Blueprint drawing of a modern flat roof with radio antenna or abstract crest."

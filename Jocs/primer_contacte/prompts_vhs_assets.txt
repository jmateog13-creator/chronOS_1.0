# PROMPTS PER A ACTIUS VISUALS - PRIMER CONTACTE (VHS MASTER STYLE)

Aquests prompts estan optimitzats per generar imatges que semblin extretes directament d'una cinta de vídeo de baixa qualitat dels anys 80.

---

### EL "SEGELL VHS" (Afegeix això a qualsevol prompt):
Keywords: `VHS tape still, VCR tracking errors, magnetic noise, scanlines, color bleeding, chromatic aberration, 4:3 aspect ratio, low bitrate, 1980s home video aesthetic, analog horror.`

---

### 1. LABORATORI SECRET (BACKGROUND)
**Prompt:** A grainy VHS still of a dark underground 1980s research facility. Distorted CRT monitors glowing with monochrome green data, analog tape recorders, retro mainframe computers. Aesthetic: found footage, heavy tracking lines at the bottom, magnetic tape noise, low resolution, desaturated colors. 

### 2. SENYAL ALIENÍGENA (SIGNAL ART)
**Prompt:** A vintage oscilloscope screen showing a complex alien signal, viewed through a bad VHS connection. Glowing green waveforms with chromatic aberration, horizontal sync jitter, VCR playback artifacts, "PLAY" text in pixelated white font at the top corner.

### 3. DOCUMENT CLASSIFICAT (ARCHIVE)
**Prompt:** A secret dossier on a desk, captured on a low-quality home video camera. Stamped "CLASSIFIED", redacted text, polaroid photos with tape distortion. The image has a slight reddish tint and fuzzy edges typical of old magnetic tape.

### 4. ICONES D'ÈPOCA (ARXIUS PER SECTOR)
Afegeix el "SEGELL VHS" a cadascun d'aquests per mantenir la coherència.

*   **ARXIU 01: MEDIEVAL (Arpa)**: A lo-fi VHS close-up of a medieval wooden celtic harp. The image is blurry, with significant color bleeding and cyan/red chromatic aberration. Framed like a secret surveillance capture.
*   **ARXIU 02: RENAIXEMENT (Llaüt)**: An analog video still of a renaissance lute on a velvet cloth. High magnetic noise, scanlines, and slight vertical jitter. 1980s home video quality.
*   **ARXIU 03: BARROC (Violí)**: A distorted VHS capture of an ornate baroque violin. Tracking errors at the bottom, fuzzy edges, and that characteristic "washed out" 80s tape color.
*   **ARXIU 04: CLASSICISME (Piano)**: A dark, grainy analog shot of a classical fortepiano. Pixelated "PLAY" text in the corner, heavy grain, and visible scanlines.
*   **ARXIU 05: ROMANTICISME (Bust de Beethoven)**: A haunting VHS still of a stone composer bust in a dark room. Deep shadows, high contrast, analog artifacts, and a slight flickering effect.
*   **ARXIU 06: SEGLE XX (Sintetitzador)**: A lo-fi capture of an early analog synthesizer with many knobs and cables. Night vision green tint, electronic interference lines, and extreme 90s tape noise.

---

### CONSELLS PER A LA GENERACIÓ:
- **Relació d'aspecte:** Si pots, demana un aspecte **4:3** per simular les pantalles antigues.
- **Acabat:** Utilitza paraules clau com "Film grain", "Chromatic aberration", "Fujicolor 1985" o "VCR noise" per obtenir el gra de la imatge.
- **Transparència:** Per a elements que vulguis superposar (com els documents), afegeix "High contrast, studio lighting, isolated on black background" per facilitar el retallat.

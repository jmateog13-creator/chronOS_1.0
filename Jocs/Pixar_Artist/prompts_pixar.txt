## 🎨 PERSONATGES (COMPOSITORS)
*Estil: Personatge 3D, fons transparent o color sòlid, posat heroic o expressiu.*

**1. GUIDO D'AREZZO (Edat Mitjana)**
> "A 3D animated character (Pixar style). A friendly medieval monk in a simple brown robe. He is holding up his hand (The Guidonian Hand) and pointing at it with a surprised and happy expression. He looks like a wise teacher. High quality render, cute and round features."

**2. HILDEGARD VON BINGEN (Edat Mitjana)**
> "A 3D animated character (Pixar style). A mystic nun with a glowing aura used for lighting. She holds a quill and an ancient book. Her eyes are looking up at the sky as if seeing visions. She looks serene, powerful, and slightly magical."

**3. PALESTRINA (Renaixement)**
> "A 3D animated character (Pixar style). A serious Renaissance composer in elegant black robes with a white stiff collar. He looks very formal and holy, holding a sheet of music that glows slightly. He has a small beard and a kind but strict face."

**4. VIVALDI (Barroc)**
> "A 3D animated character (Pixar style). A tall, thin priest with wild bright red hair (messy curls). He holds a violin and a bow dynamically. He looks energetic, as if conducting the wind. Green eyes, lively expression, wearing 18th-century priest clothes."

**5. J.S. BACH (Barroc)**
> "A 3D animated character (Pixar style). An elderly but energetic man with a massive white powdered wig that looks soft and fluffy. He wears a formal 18th-century coat. He holds a rolled-up music sheet like a baton. He has a strict but kind expression, big round eyes."

**6. MOZART (Classicisme)**
> "A 3D animated character (Pixar style). A hyper-energetic child prodigy (approx 8-10 years old) wearing a colorful Rococo velvet suit (red or purple). He has a small white wig with a ponytail. He is laughing or smiling mischievously. Very expressive face, full of life."

**7. BEETHOVEN (Romanticisme)**
> "A 3D animated character (Pixar style). A grumpy but lovable man with wild, messy grey hair. He wears a red scarf and a rumpled coat. He has a hearing trumpet in one hand. His expression is intense and dramatic (eyebrows furrowed) but cute."

**8. CHOPIN (Romanticisme)**
> "A 3D animated character (Pixar style). A handsome but pale and fragile young man. He looks poetic, sad, and romantic. He wears a fancy 19th-century suit. He is looking at a rose or holding his hand to his chest emotionally."

**9. WAGNER (Romanticisme)**
> "A 3D animated character (Pixar style). An epic, boisterous man with a velvet beret and a confident smirk. He looks ambitious and dramatic. He is holding a small toy viking helmet with horns. Big personality."

**10. JOHN CAGE (Segle XX)**
> "A 3D animated character (Pixar style). A modern man in a suit, looking confused and scratching his head. He is holding a mushroom or a stopwatch. He looks quirky, eccentric, and philosophical. Fun expression."

## 🎬 ESCENARIS (SITUACIONS)
*Estil: Fons d'escenari 3D (Environment Art), il·luminació dramàtica, sense personatges (els afegirà el jugador).*

**1. ESCENA: EL MONESTIR (Guido)**
> "A 3D Pixar-style environment background. Inside a medieval stone monastery classroom. Sunlight streaming through a small window. Wooden benches and a blackboard with musical notes. Cozy, warm, and dusty atmosphere."

**2. ESCENA: LA CEL·LA MÍSTICA (Hildegard)**
> "A 3D Pixar-style environment background. A small stone cell in a convent. A desk with candles and scrolls. A magical beam of light coming from the ceiling illuminating the desk. Mystical and purple/gold tones."

**3. ESCENA: EL VATICÀ (Palestrina)**
> "A 3D Pixar-style environment background. A grand Renaissance hall in the Vatican. Marble columns, red velvet curtains, and a high painted ceiling. Golden light. Looks majestic and religious."

**4. ESCENA: L'ORFENAT / NATURA (Vivaldi)**
> "A 3D Pixar-style environment background. A Venetian balcony overlooking a canal, but stylized with leaves blowing in the wind (representing the Four Seasons). Bright blue sky, colorful flowers, vibrant and energetic."

**5. ESCENA: L'ORGUE GEGANT (Bach)**
> "A 3D Pixar-style environment background. A close up of a massive church pipe organ. Thousands of silver pipes reaching the ceiling. Intricate wooden carvings. Mathematical symmetry. Golden hour lighting."

**6. ESCENA: PALAU ROCOCÓ (Mozart)**
> "A 3D Pixar-style environment background. A luxurious royal palace room. Pink and gold walls, crystal chandeliers, a shiny polished floor. A grand piano in the center. Very bright, pastel colors, candy-like aesthetic."

**7. ESCENA: SALA DE CONCERTS CAÒTICA (Beethoven)**

**8. ESCENA: SALÓ DE PARÍS (Chopin)**

**9. ESCENA: ESCENARI D'ÒPERA (Wagner)**

**10. ESCENA: SALA DEL SILENCI (John Cage)**

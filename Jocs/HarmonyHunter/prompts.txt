## 🎵 MÚSICA (Doom-Style + Història)

La idea és barrejar **Heavy Metal/Industrial (Estil Mick Gordon/Doom)** amb **instruments d'època**.

**Instruccions generals per a IA de Música (Suno/Udio):**
"Heavy Metal, Industrial Metal, High Tempo, Aggressive, Video Game Soundtrack, Loopable."

### Prompts Específics per Època:

**1. Edat Mitjana (Medieval)**
> "Heavy Metal mixed with medieval lute and flute, aggressive drums, doom style soundtrack, dark gregorian chants in background."

**2. Renaixement**
> "Speed metal mixed with harpsichord and chamber music, neoclassical shredding, fast tempo, double kick drum, intense renaissance fair gone wrong."

**3. Barroc**
> "Industrial metal mixed with massive church pipe organ, toccata and fugue style, dramatic, gothic atmosphere, heavy distortion."

**4. Classicisme**
> "Symphonic metal mixed with Mozart and Haydn melodies, orderly classical strings meeting aggressive distorted guitars, double kick pedals, refined but brutal."

**5. Romanticisme**
> "Symphonic metal, chaotic orchestra, violent violin solos, operatic vocals (screaming/singing), emotional and heavy, wagnerian style."

**6. Segle XX (Jazz/Industrial)**
> "Djent metal mixed with chaotic free jazz saxophones, dissonant, odd time signatures, mechanical industrial sounds, war atmosphere."

**7. Actualitat (Digital/Glitch)**
> "Glitch metal, dubstep breakdowns, cybernetic sounds, 8-bit synthesizer solos mixed with heavy down-tuned guitars, futuristic and digital."

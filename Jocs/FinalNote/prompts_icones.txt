# Prompts per a Icones d'Horror (Final Note)

Aquí tens els prompts que he fet servir per crear els avatars "malrolleros" d'estil SAW. Tots estan pensats per ser icones quadrades sobre fons negre:

1. **Màscara de Ferro**: "A gritty, rusty iron mask with sharp edges and dark shadows, cinematic lighting, SAW horror style, isolated on black background, square icon."
2. **Crani Mecànic**: "A stylized skull made of twisted metal wires and rusted parts, glowing dim red eyes, industrial horror style, square icon, black background."
3. **Cara de Porcellana**: "A shattered porcelain doll face, glitched and distorted, dark cracks, horror aesthetic, isolated on black background, square icon."
4. **Engranatge Roig**: "A heavy, rusty mechanical gear covered in dried dark blood, industrial torture device aesthetic, isolated on black background, square icon."
5. **Màscara de Sac**: "A dirty, blood-stained burlap sack mask, stitched eyes with thick black thread, horror movie style, sinister, isolated on black background, square icon."
6. **Respirador Corrosiu**: "A cracked vintage medical respirator, rusted and dirty, dark noxious smoke leaking out, horror aesthetic, square icon, black background."
7. **Titella Sinistra**: "A sinister wooden puppet mask, strings visible on the sides, glowing dim yellow eyes, horror style, square icon, black background."

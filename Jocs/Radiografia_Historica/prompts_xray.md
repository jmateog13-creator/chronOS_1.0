# Prompts per a Generar els Assets de Radiografia (X-Ray Art)

Utilitza aquests prompts en una eina com Midjourney, DALL-E 3 o Leonardo.ai.
**Estil General:** "X-ray imagery style, blue and black palette, glowing cyan medical imaging, semi-transparent bones and objects, high contrast, scientific medical illustration style, isolated on black background."

---

## 1. Siluetes de Personatges (El Cos del Pacient)
Genera una imatge de fons per a cada època. Ha de ser una silueta humana (esquelet visible estil raigs X) però amb un accessori identificatiu de l'època.
*Prompt Base:* "Full body medical x-ray of a human skeleton, front view, cyan glowing bones on black background... [ACCESSORY DETAIL]"

1.  **Edat Mitjana:** "...wearing a metal knight helmet visible in x-ray."
2.  **Renaixement:** "...wearing a renaissance painter beret cap visible in x-ray."
3.  **Barroc:** "...wearing a large voluminous baroque wig visible in x-ray."
4.  **Classicisme:** "...wearing a neat ponytail wig and coat outline visible in x-ray."
5.  **Romanticisme:** "...with wild messy Beethoven-style hair visible in x-ray."
6.  **Segle XX:** "...wearing headphones or sunglasses visible in x-ray."

# Pentagrama Runner - Asset Generation Prompts (Rubber Hose / Cuphead Style)

**General Style Guide:**
- **Style:** 1930s Rubber Hose Animation ("Cuphead" style).
- **Aesthetic:** Hand-drawn, vintage look, film grain texture, watercolor backgrounds, expressive "pie eyes", noodle limbs, constant bouncing motion.
- **Palette:** Muted vintage technicolor or sepia tones, but with distinct color themes for each era as specified (backgrounds).
- **Mood:** Surreal, energetic, jazz-inspired, slightly spooky or zany.

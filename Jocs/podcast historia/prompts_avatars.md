# Prompts per als Avatars del Podcast del Temps

Perquè tots els avatars tinguin el mateix estil visual (estil "Premium Concept Art"), utilitza aquesta estructura de prompt base i canvia només el personatge.

### Estil Base (Master Prompt)
> "A professional 2D avatar portrait of **[PERSONATGE]** for a podcast guest. Modern studio lighting feel. Flat background, clean edges, premium digital art style. High quality, detailed facial features. Transparent background."

---

### Propostes d'Avatars (Mix d'Homes i Dones)

1. **Edat Mitjana (Dona - Trobadairitz)**
   > "A professional 2D avatar portrait of a Medieval Troubadairitz (female troubadour) for a podcast guest. She is wearing a simple headpiece and a medieval gown. Modern studio lighting feel. Flat background, clean edges, premium digital art style. Transparent background."

2. **Renaixement (Home - Savi/Compositor)**
   > "A professional 2D avatar portrait of a Renaissance scholar for a podcast guest. He has a scholarly beard, wearing a velvet doublet and a flat cap. Modern studio lighting feel. Flat background, clean edges, premium digital art style. Transparent background."

3. **Barroc (Dona - Compositora de l'època)**
   > "A professional 2D avatar portrait of a Baroque female musician (like Barbara Strozzi) for a podcast guest. She is wearing an elegant 17th-century dress with lace details and her hair is styled with pearls. Modern studio lighting feel. Flat background, clean edges, premium digital art style. Transparent background."

4. **Classicisme (Home - Elegant vienès)**
   > "A professional 2D avatar portrait of an 18th-century elegant gentleman (Mozart style) for a podcast guest. Clean shaven, light wig, wearing a silk waistcoat. Modern studio lighting feel. Flat background, clean edges, premium digital art style. Transparent background."

5. **Romanticisme (Dona - Pianista apassionada)**
   > "A professional 2D avatar portrait of a passionate 19th-century Romantic female artist (Clara Schumann style) for a podcast guest. Dark Victorian dress, expressive eyes, hair tied in a bun. Modern studio lighting feel. Flat background, clean edges, premium digital art style. Transparent background."

6. **Segle XX (Home - Experimentalista/Jazz)**
   > "A professional 2D avatar portrait of a 20th-century music experimentalist for a podcast guest. 1950s suit, wearing thick glasses and holding a classic microphone. Modern studio lighting feel. Flat background, clean edges, premium digital art style. Transparent background."

---

### Consells per a la consistència:
- Si fas servir **Midjourney**, afegeix `--no photo` per mantenir l'estil d'il·lustració 2D.
- Si fas servir **DALL-E 3**, menciona "vector style" o "flat digital art" si veus que es tornen massa realistes.
- El paràmetre `Transparent background` ajuda molt a retallar les imatges després per al joc.

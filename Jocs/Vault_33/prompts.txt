# VAULT 33: ASSET GENERATION PROMPTS

## General Style Note
All assets should follow the iconic "Fallout" retro-futuristic aesthetic. 
Characters: Use the "Vault Boy" cartoon style OR the realistic "Fallout 4/76" concept art style.
Backgrounds: High-detail 2D environment art, 16:9 aspect ratio, industrial/atomic-age aesthetic.
